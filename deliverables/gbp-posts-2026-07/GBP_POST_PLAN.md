## Upload notes

- The prepared JPGs are 1500–2000 px on their longest side, correctly oriented, and each is well under Google’s 5 MB photo limit.
- `images/10-brunette-half-up-formal-style.jpg` is an unused reserve image; the sharper, more varied textured updo is scheduled for Post 10.
- `images/05-brunette-braided-updo.jpg` is an unused duplicate export retained only as a backup; use the correctly named half-up image listed for Post 5.
- Use the post text exactly as written; the action belongs in the GBP button rather than as a pasted URL in the body.
- Do not add a phone number to the post text. Google advises using the profile’s call button instead.
- Skip hashtag blocks and repeated city/service lists. They add clutter and make the posts read like keyword stuffing.
- Keep the Business Profile’s services, hours, categories, photos, and business details complete and accurate. Posts support customer engagement and relevance, but Google says local ranking primarily depends on relevance, distance, and prominence.
